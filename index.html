01 About

Connecting rigorous theory with consequential systems.

Song Chong is the ICT Endowed Chair Professor and Head of the Kim Jaechul Graduate School of Artificial Intelligence at KAIST. He is also an adjunct professor in KAIST’s School of Electrical Engineering and College of Business, and at New York University.

He is Founding Director of the KAIST Edge AI Research Center and a member of Korea’s Presidential Council on National AI Strategy. Before joining academia, he was a Member of Technical Staff at AT&T Bell Laboratories.

02 Research

Research interests

Methods for making better decisions in dynamic, uncertain, and interconnected environments.

01

Decision making under uncertainty

Sequential decisions, reinforcement learning, and robust control when information is incomplete or changes over time.

02

Human behavior modelling

Understanding mobility and behavior patterns, and their implications for intelligent networked systems.

03

Optimization and machine learning

Mathematical foundations and learning-based methods for resource allocation, scheduling, and system control.

04

AI, edge systems, and networks

AI-native networks, mobile computing, edge intelligence, and reliable high-performance data infrastructure.
